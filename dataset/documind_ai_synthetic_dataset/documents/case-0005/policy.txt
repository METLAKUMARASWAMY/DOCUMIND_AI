Finance Payment and Compliance Policy
All invoices must reference an approved purchase order.
Invoice amount must match the approved purchase order amount unless finance approves an exception.
New vendor bank account requests must be verified through an independent channel before payment.
Contracts must include confidentiality, indemnity, termination, governing law, and audit rights clauses.
Healthcare vendors handling PHI require a HIPAA Business Associate Addendum.
High-risk compliance findings or fraud signals must block automatic approval.
