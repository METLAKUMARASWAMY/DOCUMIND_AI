K. ANOMALY AND RISK ANALYSIS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Use layered risk analysis.

Layer 1 — Deterministic business rules:

- subtotal + tax - discount approximately equals total
- due date must not precede invoice date
- duplicate invoice number must be checked
- vendor account must match approved master data
- invoice vendor must match PO vendor
- invoice amount must be within PO tolerance
- required signature or approval must be present
- contract must be active on invoice date
- currency must be consistent
- tax identifier must have a valid format
- invoice payment terms must not conflict with contract terms

Layer 2 — Unsupervised anomaly detection:

Use Isolation Forest when labelled data is unavailable.

Features may include:

- vendor amount z-score
- amount-to-average ratio
- invoice frequency
- time since previous invoice
- bank-account change frequency
- validation-failure count
- OCR uncertainty
- document similarity
- unusual submission time
- number of vendors sharing an account

Layer 3 — Supervised risk model:

Use XGBoost only when sufficient labelled data exists.

Evaluate using:

- precision
- recall
- F1
- AUPRC
- false-positive rate
- recall at fixed review capacity

Use SHAP for model explanations.

Layer 4 — Graph anomaly analysis:

Use GraphSAGE or GAT only after sufficient connected and labelled graph data
exists.

Do not train a GNN using isolated invoice images.
