# Research Novelty

DocuMind AI goes beyond OCR by converting document text into evidence-grounded enterprise decisions.

## Multi-Document Reasoning
The system compares contract, invoice, purchase order, email, bank statement, and policy records to detect mismatches and workflow blockers.

## Evidence-Grounded RAG
Answers are not free-form guesses. Each answer includes document, page, chunk ID, confidence, and evidence text.

The payout intent path demonstrates structured retrieval: instead of returning raw chunks, it directly answers invoice amount, PO amount, match status, payment terms, due date, approval status, blockers, evidence, and required action.

## Compliance Intelligence
The platform detects missing indemnity, confidentiality, termination, governing law, audit rights, policy mismatch, and regulatory risk.

## Fraud Detection
The MVP detects bank account change requests, urgent payment wording, manual overrides, duplicate invoices, amount mismatches, and vendor anomalies.

## Explainability
Findings, agents, workflow, reports, and graph relationships all link back to evidence.

## Workflow Automation
High-risk cases route to hold payment, request changes, or escalation instead of unsafe approval.

## Enterprise Readiness
The submission includes local authentication, role badges, protected decisions and reports, SHA-256 checksums, audit trails, environment-based secrets, Docker, Kubernetes manifests, CI, and evaluator-focused documentation.
